# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Current residents**: Coyle Hall men who use the site as a reference point for staff, government, events, and hall life. Casual, repeat visitors who know the hall.
- **Incoming students and families**: Prospective and newly admitted Notre Dame students discovering what Coyle Hall is about. First impressions count; they are evaluating community fit.
- **Parents**: Visiting for Parents Weekend or seeking staff and contact info. They want trust signals and clear orientation.
- **Hall government members**: Using the site as a reference for commissioners, governance structure, and official information.

Context: visited on phones at ND, on laptops during move-in research, on family devices during fall visit season.

## Product Purpose

The official website for Coyle Hall at the University of Notre Dame. Its job is to tell the hall's story — its history (rooted in Fisher Hall), its traditions (Regatta, Car Smash, Funk, Formal, Faith), its people (staff, RAs, hall government), and the practical information incoming residents need. Success means a visitor understands who Coyle Hall is and feels the community's character, not just its org chart.

## Brand Personality

Brotherhood, tradition, pride — first. Welcoming, active, grounded underneath. The hall has institutional weight (a long Fisher/Coyle history, deep ND roots) and genuine community warmth. It earns seriousness through history, not performance. Voice is direct and proud, never stiff or self-promotional.

## Anti-references

- **Generic university web template**: The safe, forgettable official ND department look — muted neutrals, stock photography, interchangeable layouts. Coyle Hall has a specific identity; the site should be unmistakably *this* hall.
- **Social media / app feel**: Card feeds, infinite scroll, Instagram-grid layouts. This is a place, not a platform. The site should feel like walking into the hall, not scrolling a feed.

## Design Principles

1. **History earns its weight**: Coyle Hall's Fisher roots and traditions are real and specific. Design gives them visual gravity — typography, photography, and layout that feel like they've been earned, not assembled.
2. **Place, not platform**: The experience should feel like entering a physical community space. Warmth and groundedness over novelty and interactivity.
3. **Identity-first palette**: Coyle green and ND identity are not decorative — they are the brand. Don't dilute them with trendy neutrals or generic color strategies.
4. **Welcoming, not recruiting**: The tone invites; it doesn't pitch. New students and families should feel oriented and welcome, not sold to.
5. **Accessible by default**: WCAG AA minimum across all surfaces, with a preference for AAA-level contrast and robust reduced-motion support wherever achievable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG AA minimum; aspire to AAA contrast ratios on body text and key UI
- Reduced-motion: honor `prefers-reduced-motion` on all animations and parallax; provide static fallbacks
- No accessibility features removed for aesthetic reasons
