Until the Crunk

Get Down, Get Groovy

An iconic tradition passed down from Fisher, the Coyle Crunk is our annual '70s themed SYR. Different from the usual dorm SYR (Screw Your Roommate), the Crunk is a chance for everyone in Coyle — and their dates — to embrace their inner '70s spirit with groovy fashion, flare pants, disco shirts, and (for the particularly brave) handlebar mustaches and long sideburns.

Each year the dorm gathers in Stepan Center for a night of roller skating, dancing, and good old-fashioned '70s fun. Lace up your skates, dust off your bell-bottoms, and get ready to boogie.
