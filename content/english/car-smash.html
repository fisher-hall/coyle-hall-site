Smashing Cars, Building Futures

As one of Coyle Hall's signature and most beloved events, the Car Smash is a fun and casual way to raise money for St. Adalbert's. Every year, the Friday before a fall home game, Coyle Hall brings an old car out to Fieldhouse Mall. For a small donation, participants have the chance to smash the car with a hammer, and even bring home their own pieces as souvenirs. Not to mention, the opponents mascot is on the car, providing the perfect opportunity for fans to express their true feelings.

With music, good food, fun vibes, and a lively atmosphere, the Car Smash is the best way to kick off a home game weekend and help support a great cause in the process!

Thanks to everyone who came out to support the Car Smash this past year, we'll see you in the Fall!
