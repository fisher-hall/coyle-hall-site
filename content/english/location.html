A community of Activity

Though the community has called several buildings home over the years, our roots have always been on South Quad, otherwise known as the longest quad in America. Near the heart of campus, the dorm is nestled between classic Notre Dame landmarks, with South Dining Hall right next door and the Rock only steps away. Next door lies Grojean Hall, to the south are Duncan, McGlinn, and O'Neill Halls, and through the Lyons Arch you can see the beautiful St. Mary's Lake. Whether it's playing spikeball or hacky sack on the quad, jogging around the lakes, joining a pickup game at the Bookstore Courts, or stopping by the dining hall for a quick bite, Coyle Hall is close to the action and near everything you need to be succesful, have fun, and enjoy college life!
