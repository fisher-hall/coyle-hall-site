A Classic Notre Dame Night

In the spring comes a classic Notre Dame dorm tradition: our formal. Usually co-hosted with a women's dorm, the Coyle Formal is the dorm's chance to dress up and enjoy our own fancy night out.

Held on campus, the formal gives the Crew the opportunity to wear their best suits and ties, bring a date, and share a memorable evening together. Keep an eye on this page — the date and venue will be announced as spring approaches.
