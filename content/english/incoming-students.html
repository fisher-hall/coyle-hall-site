Welcome Incoming Students!

Congrats! You've been chosen to become part of the best dorm on campus. We couldn't be more excited to welcome you to your new home, and we hope you're looking forward to it as much as we are. This isn’t just where you’ll live—it’s where you’ll grow, laugh, make unforgettable memories, and find a community that truly feels like home. Get excited for a warm welcome, incredible people, and the beginning of something truly special. We can’t wait to meet you and see everything you’ll bring to our community.

As you prepare for move in, check out the resources we've compiled below to help you get ready. Below, you'll find room information, packing lists, and other important details to help you make the most of your time in Coyle. Additionally, take some time to reach out to your roomate(s) and get to know them (you will be living together after all). Hopefully, you'll become good friends!

What's in a Room

All first-year students are placed in standard size doubles (non-deluxe), or accesible singles/doubles if they have an accommodation. Transfer students may be placed in any type of room, though it is usually a standard double or single. All rooms have windows, sinks, A/C and a standard set of furniture. Accesible rooms may have private bathrooms as well as accesible furniture. Check out standard room furnishings below:

Sinks are on a cabinet with shelves below and a medicine cabinet and mirror above.

Things to Bring, Pack, or Buy

This is an incomplete list, but here are some common items to consider having in your room. You'll want to coordinate with your roommate, especially with bigger items, so you don't end up bringing two of everything.

There are plenty of stores in the South Bend area, including Target, Costco, and Walmart, if you'd rather buy stuff after move-in. Feel free to have items delivered to your room, but they must arrive after move-in or they won't be accepted.
