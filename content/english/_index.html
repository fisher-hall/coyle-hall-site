Fishers of Men

1952 • 2025

Crew. Fishermen. Green Wave. Brothers. Coyle Hall has been known by many names over the years, but its legacy as one of the most iconic dorms at Notre Dame remains steadfast.

Home to generations of proud residents, this brotherhood has long been one of the most tight-knit, spirited, and iconic on campus. From legendary events and unmatched camaraderie, to unforgettable haircuts and unwavering support, Coyle Hall isn't just a place for students to reside — it's a community of friendships, traditions, and countless memories that last a lifetime. Students come from every major, from every corner of the world and from every background, united by their shared identity as the Crew, and the bonds forged here are unlike any other.

The mantra of "All are welcome" has carried through since the community's inception in 1952 as Fisher Hall, and it is even more true now, in the newest (and nicest) dorm on campus. The Cadillac, as the dorm is affectionately known, is a place for students to work, relax, study, grow, pray, but above all live at Notre Dame, and we couldn't be more happy to welcome you to our home!
