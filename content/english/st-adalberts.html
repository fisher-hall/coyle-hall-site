A Community of Care

Coyle Hall has been blessed with the opportunity to give back to the South Bend community by supporting St. Adalbert's Catholic School. Each year, the dorm raises funds through our signature events, including the Regatta, Car Smash, and more. Money raised by the dorm goes directly to the school, helping to fund improvement projects, school programs, and other initiatives benefiting students in our community. Beyond the money, the Crew show up in person, volunteering and spending time supporting the school and its students.

The relationship with St. Adalbert's is built on the qualities of community and charity that are central to Coyle Hall, and it is the foundation of everything we do as a dorm.

About St. Adalbert's

St. Adalbert's is a Catholic School located in western South Bend and operating by the Congregation of Holy Cross. As both are Holy Cross schools, Notre Dame and St. Adalbert's share a special relationship, one that Coyle Hall is proud to be a part of. Priests from both institutions frequently visit each other and collaborate across education, ministry, and service.

St. Adalbert's has over 200 students ranging from Pre-K to 8th grade. The school and parish are home to large Hispanic and Latino communities, and the school offers bilingual education and support for students. The school opened in 1911 and was originally ministered by the Felician Sisters from Detroit. In 2003, ministry of the parish and school was transferred to the Congregation of Holy Cross, where it has remained through today.
