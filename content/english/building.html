A Community of Comfort

Built in 2026, Coyle Hall is one of the newest dorms on campus and features all the modern amenities and comforts you could ask for in a residence hall. Each floor has a slightly different layout, but they each have all the essentials. You'll find spacious lounges, complete with a full kitchen with dishes and utensils, a TV, couches, study spaces, and more.

The building is home to a variety of room types, including singles, doubles, three-room quads, and three-room six-man rooms, accessible singles and doubles, as well as apartments for hall staff. A small handful of doubles and singles have private bathrooms, but most rooms share between three large communal bathrooms, as well as some individual restrooms.

The dorm is broken up into eight sections across the three floors. The first floor has two sections, while the second and third both have three. On the first floor, you'll also find a number of common spaces with game tables and ammenities for the whole dorm. The St. Paul's Chapel is located on the east side of the building, along with a spacious reading room and various study spaces. The first floor also houses common areas and lounges, as well as offices for hall staff and apartments for our rector as well as our Priest-in-Residence. In the basement, you'll find laundry and storage, our dorm gym, which features free weights, treadmill, cable machine, bench, squat rack, and more, plus our very own dorm sandwich shop, Ellie's Deli! Outside the dorm you'll find a large patio for communal gatherings and grilling, as well as a large green space for outdoor activities and games.

The Chapel

The Chapel of St. Paul, located on the first floor of Coyle Hall, is a holy place where the community gathers for Mass and prayer. Each week, the Crew come together to celebrate Sunday mass and daily mass throughout the week, complete with homemade ice cream on Wednesdays. The chapel is one of the largest on campus, and the piano and instruments inside enable the Coyle Dorm Choir to sing and perform at masses.

In addition to masses and sacraments, the chapel is also a meeting place for the dorm community, where we come together to meet as a community.

Section Map

The Hall is divided into 8 sections, with two on the first floor and three on the second and third. While Coyle Hall is proudly an anti-section culture dorm (which means we emphasize community across the entire dorm), sections are a nice supplement to dorm culture. Each section has an RA, residents across all four grades, and some even do special events. This map shows the naming layout for each section. The part of the building and the floor combine to make section names (ie. 2B is the middle section on the second floor). The first floor has two sections, 1A and 1B, while the second and third floors each have three sections, A, B, and C.
