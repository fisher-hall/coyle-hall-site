Welcome Home, Coyle Families

Welcome to campus, Coyle families! There is no better time to be under the Dome than the weekend the Irish take on Miami, and we cannot wait to share it with you. Family Weekend is our chance to pull back the curtain on hall life, introduce you to the Crew, and celebrate the community your sons call home. Get ready for a weekend packed with game-day energy, gold-and-blue pride, and the warm Coyle spirit you have heard so much about.

We kick things off Friday evening with an opening dinner, gathering the Crew and their families together to start the weekend on a full and festive note. From there, the campus and the game-day atmosphere take over, and you will have plenty of time to make memories with your son and other families. On Sunday morning, we come back together for a closing Mass, followed by a farewell brunch to send everyone off with full hearts and plates. We are so glad you are here, and we cannot wait to welcome you home to Coyle.

  November 6–8, 2026

  Notre Dame vs. Miami
